Descripción:
The image contrasts third-party and first-party cookies, explaining their differences and functions.

- Third-party cookies
  - Track user behavior between multiple sites
  - Used for retargeting and ad-serving
  - Created and owned by third-party servers
  - Accessible on any website with the server's code

- First-party cookies
  - Store user information specific to the site
  - Improve user experience
  - Created and owned by the domain/site
  - Accessible only on the domain that created them

This illustration clarifies the distinctions and implications of using third-party versus first-party cookies in web browsing.
