Descripción:
This image illustrates the process of third-party cookie retargeting.

- User visits website A
- Website A sends back cookies with unique ID
- User exits A and visits website B
- Cookies from A are stored on the user's browser
- User clicks on an ad from B and returns to A

The image explains how third-party cookies enable websites to remember users across different sites, allowing for targeted advertising.
